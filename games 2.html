About Us

TechTrash is a website created to spread awareness about electronic waste and its impact on our planet. Our goal is to help young people understand why e-waste is a problem and how they can make a difference. Through simple explanations, useful facts, images, quizzes and games, we make learning about e-waste fun and interesting. We believe that small actions, such as reusing, repairing and recycling electronics, can help create a cleaner and healthier environment. Join TechTrash and become part of the solution!
